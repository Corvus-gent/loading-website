How leads and offers are ranked

Ranked by expected value (mid-budget in EUR × estimated close chance), then urgency. Warm relationships boost close chance. Unknown budgets stay unpriced; ask before building.

Score: pay 30, urgency 25, trust 25, fit 10, reachability 10. Red flags subtract points and reduce close chance. FX comes from Frankfurter / ECB; stale or missing rates are never guessed. Hourly rates use an explicit scope-hours estimate, shown per lead. A missing Maps website is a prospect, not proof of buying intent.
