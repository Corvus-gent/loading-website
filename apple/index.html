02 · the research I want to do

Every layer, one question each.

What I've built or written, and the question it left me with. Course projects are labelled as course projects.

Operating systems

Where does the model live?

Corvus Noctis puts a conversational helper inside the shell, running locally in the browser through WebGPU, or against a local model server.

Question: when an assistant is part of the OS, which layer should own it: the kernel, the shell, or each app?

Hardware & robotics

Machines in ordinary rooms.

PartyCrasher 3000, a robot that drives, dances, talks and serves drinks (Python, ROS 2). Master's coursework in robotics and machine learning.

Question: what does a device need to feel trustworthy when it moves through someone's home?

Machine learning · human–AI

Help, or quiet steering?

Mutual Manipulation Between LLMs and Humans (course research, 2025) and physics-informed deep learning in medical imaging (course research, team of five).

Question: how do you measure whether an assistant improves someone's judgement, rather than just their speed?

Privacy

Useful without knowing you.

My own site counts visits without tracking cookies, drops to anonymous totals when Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track is on, and lists the few things it stores, which expire. Read the policy, written to be read.

Question: how much product insight survives when you refuse to know anything about individuals?

Brand & philosophy

A small brand, built in the open.

Corvus has a logo the community votes on, an interactive explainer on the maths of a Rubik's cube, and a page written for each person I want to reach, this one included. I tell the story in seven languages. My route is turning from computer science toward philosophy: knowledge, agency and responsibility stopped being abstract once I built with AI every day.

Question: what makes people love an object, and how do you protect that while a company grows around it?
