no mystery score. here's the maths. ✨

TikTok and Instagram: plays × 1 + likes × 3 + comments × 8 + shares × 10 + saves × 5. Instagram shares and saves require owner Insights.

X: impressions × 1 + likes × 3 + replies × 8 + (reposts + quotes) × 10 + bookmarks × 5.

LinkedIn: video views or impressions × 1 + reactions × 3 + comments × 8 + reposts × 10 + saves × 5. Use one reach metric. Owner entries are self-reported.

YouTube: views × 1 + likes × 3 + comments × 8 + owner-reported shares × 10. Saves are not exposed. Twitch: views × 1; no comparable per-video likes, comments, shares or saves.

Velocity = reported score ÷ hours since posting, using measurements from the first 24 hours only. If we don't have that measurement or a posting time, we show —. No lifetime score divided by 24 pretending to be a first-day result.

0 is a measured zero. — means a missing measurement; n/a means the platform does not expose the metric. A partial score is the sum of known terms only. X “views” are impressions; shares include reposts + quotes. Engagements = likes + comments + shares + saves.
