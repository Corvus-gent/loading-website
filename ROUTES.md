## Host and dynamic routes

- `stats.corvus.gent/` maps to `/stats/`; its named sections map to the corresponding stats pages. Main-host `/stats/*` redirects to that subdomain.
- `/people/?id=<member>` selects a member; `/links/<persona>` and `/go/<persona>/<platform>/<context>` resolve dynamic profile/outbound links.
- `/join-the-army-now` remains a production-only recruitment flow owned by open PR #18; this preview links explicitly to the production host. No duplicate account system was created.
- `/sf/`, `/stats/private/`, `/stats/hunt/`, `/music/admin/`, client concepts, and noindex Airbnb contact pages remain unlisted. Existing access checks are unchanged.
- Photo sources: `public/hack/2/floor.jpg`, `slide.jpg`, `setup.jpg`; absent photos render placeholders.
