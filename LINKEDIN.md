## 2. The four-hour test

Luma is sinking. I have four hours. Convince me to get on the boat.

The broke hacker needs food, a cheap way to get there, and an answer on how prizes pay out. The busy builder needs a possible co-founder. The connector needs three warm intros. The investor wants to see a working product and someone using it.

Same event. Four completely different decisions.

I built The Hackers Bay for Boat Hackathon Demo Day. Switch lenses and watch the same fictional room give you different reasons to go and different people to meet.

Try it at https://corvus.gent/hackersbay and tell me which piece of information you’d want before saying yes to an event.

## 3. The better invite

Luma is sinking. Long live the two-way intro.

Imagine getting an invite that says, “She has a prototype and needs a backend co-founder. You’re looking for someone who can talk to customers.” Then imagine both of you getting to say yes before anyone puts a meeting on your calendar.

That’s the idea behind The Hackers Bay. We built a demo for Boat Hackathon in SF, down to a sonar, a room composer for hosts, and a treasure report for sponsors. The attendee roster is fictional, so you can play through the whole intro in a minute.

Try it at https://corvus.gent/hackersbay and tell me what would earn a yes from you.
