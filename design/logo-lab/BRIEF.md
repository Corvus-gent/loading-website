# The crow at the door

The homepage is a black field with a single invitation, “one man. an army of builders.” Five doors lead into Quests, Daily, Legion, Live and Gabe. The site shell uses monospace, grey ink and lantern gold. The existing square mark is a crowned white bird with a heavy eye, perched inside a black tile. The tile carries a lot of its visual weight.

Legion gives the first rank for $1 and asks members to earn the rest. SF LARPing is deliberately personal. HackTheFeed mixes a live signal with game graphics. The crow flying toward the lantern in img/diogenes.svg suggests curiosity before conquest. The site can speak both very quietly and very loudly.

Five bets: turn the beak into a command, give the Legion a field standard, hand the lantern to a street printer, cut a bird from a letter, or build a sprite that could inhabit the stream. Compare at equal optical size. A simplified favicon may shed detail, but must retain the silhouette. Use the existing Redis connection for picks and static directory routing for the lab. Keep the homepage experiment inside the lab.
