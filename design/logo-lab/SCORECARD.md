**Flyer** puts the lantern in the crow’s beak. Test whether people still notice what it carries on a two-inch sticker. Try a swinging lantern with a shorter handle and open up the gap between the legs.

**Sigil** wins the browser tab. The eye and open counter survive at 16 px. Try a more hooked bill and test unprompted recognition against the present cut. The main risk is reading it as a duck.

**Prompt** feels native to the terminal. The heavy diagonal also resembles a key. **Legio** works best as a rank patch, where the banner and laurel have room to breathe. **Arcade** makes a lively Twitch avatar, but the high brow and short tail read younger than the brand.

## Two passes

The initial, optical and character boards in rounds/ preserve the artwork at each stage. The first pass increased Prompt’s stroke weight, strengthened Legio’s crossbar, attached Flyer’s lantern, enlarged Sigil’s eye and adjusted Arcade’s eye pixels. The second separated Prompt’s cursor from the body, added Legio’s laurels, cut a flame into Flyer’s lamp, split Sigil’s bill and gave Arcade a pair of signal pixels.
