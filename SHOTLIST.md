# The Hackers Bay shoot

Gabe can film these with a phone before or during Demo Day. The Remotion clips cover the cannonball, sonar, and end card. Use the phone screen captures for the actual product steps.

| Shot | Frame and action | Audio or caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Hook | Vertical selfie with water behind you. Get right to the line, then glance toward the dock. | “Luma is sinking.” |
| 2. Place | Film the Historic Klamath from the pier. Hold a stable wide for three seconds, then get a close-up of a cleat, rope, or gangway. | “We built this for a hackathon on a boat.” |
| 3. Friction | Record the Hackers Bay landing and scroll to the comparison. Hold the wall of fictional avatars. Use the Remotion cannonball as the cut. | “Which of these people is worth four hours?” |
| 4. Boarding | Screen record at phone width. Tap **Board the ship**, paste a public demo link, and tap **Draft my card**. Hold the Builds, Wants, and Offers fields as the edit points. | “One public link. One crew card.” |
| 5. The lens | Switch between Broke hacker and Busy builder. Show the score, then rack focus or cut to the sonar locks. | “Same boat. Different reasons to go.” |
| 6. Three people | Tap the first radar lock. Let **Why you’d meet** and **Why they’d meet** both read clearly. | “Three people. A reason on both sides.” |
| 7. Mutual | Record **Intro me** and the pending state. Cut to the fictional recipient view as you tap **Accept intro**. Hold the mutual state and its afternoon meetup. | Let the phonk drop carry “IT’S MUTUAL ⚓.” |
| 8. Host and sponsor | Record a person moving into Room composer, then one filter switch in Treasure report. Use in the 30s and 60s versions. | “Hosts build a room. Sponsors find a pilot.” |
| 9. Sponsor features | Optional 60s insert. Show Private mode, then open the labeled Proof of Build sample from the crew card. | “Context reads the profile, NYTEX routes private mode, Boat powers sandbox previews.” |
| 10. Close | Finish on a portrait of you at the pier, then the Remotion end card with the full URL. | “RSVP to people, not places. Try it and tell me what breaks.” |

Record vertical and horizontal takes of shots 1, 2, and 10. Keep the phone UI within the center of the frame for reframing. Use fictional demo profiles and capture only people who agree to be filmed. Record a clean take of each line plus a few seconds of water and deck ambience.
