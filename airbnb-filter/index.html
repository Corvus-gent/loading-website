Me vs. the Airbnb filter

Thursday morning, 5 Nov 2026. ~20 minutes.

I wanted to book a place for the same afternoon. I had one quick question for the host. A 30-second phone call would have fixed it.

Airbnb disagreed.

attempt 1  phone number, raw             -> blocked
attempt 2  "0 four 7 eight ..."          -> blocked
attempt 3  "0 four 787 four 1911"        -> blocked

Fine. If I can't send my number, I'll send a website with my number on it.

So I shipped corvus.gent/airbnb-call-me-pls (number redacted here, sorry internet): one page, one big "call me" button, available in all 7 languages I speak. Pushed to production in under 10 minutes.

attempt 4  a link to my site              -> blocked
attempt 5  the link, spelled out          -> blocked
attempt 6  "just google it"               -> blocked
"Links and contact info can't be shared until after a reservation is confirmed."

Score: Airbnb 6, Gabriel 1. One message did slip through to a host.

What I learned

- their filter is good. not perfect.
- shipping a page is faster than arguing with a chatbox
- sometimes the hack is just: book, then call

The page is still live. A monument to stubbornness.
