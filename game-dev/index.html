what the background is doing (move your cursor, drag, or use arrow keys)

Screen-space dither. The dot pattern is glued to the screen, so moving water shimmers and swims.

Surface-stable dither. The pattern is glued to the world, so it travels with the water and reads like print.

Background: the scene is rendered, sampled per cell and drawn as glyphs. Light and its reflection on the water pick denser characters; glyphs only change when brightness clearly crosses a level, so nothing flickers.
