F. Gabriel Van Langenhove

Computer scientist, entrepreneur and aspiring philosopher. I want to study philosophy at KU Leuven.

About me

I’m Gabriel. I speak seven human languages and a few programming languages. I studied computer science at Ghent University for five years and came close to finishing my master’s. These days, my work often starts with an idea I want to try out quickly. What I do best is use Codex and Claude to turn that idea into a working prototype, then test and refine it. I program in languages including Python, Rust and C, and have worked with ROS 2.

I founded Corvus, a community of builders working entirely in public, with a focus on quality. I dropped out to work at a start-up in Ghent. From early to mid-November 2026, I was in the United States: I attended hackathons and spoke with founders in San Francisco and New York.

I ran a tutoring business. The subjects that interested me most were Introduction to Programming, Data Science, algorithms and basic mathematics.

I am a competitive Brazilian jiu-jitsu athlete and train six to ten times a week. I want to compete frequently, including in IBJJF competitions.

Why I stopped studying computer science

The programme no longer sparked my curiosity. The pace felt too slow for me, and I did not always agree with how the courses were taught. I felt I had stopped learning: I completed assignments with AI without learning much from them and could pass exams with very little studying.

Now I build things myself and learn much more about computer science at my own pace. I want to understand what I make, try it out and improve it.

Why philosophy

I philosophise all day. At every step in my life, I think about how things could be better. I use AI to explore my thoughts, sharpen my questions and think through my choices.

I have many creative ideas, and AI finally lets me express and develop them. I also want to learn how to investigate the questions behind them rigorously, through careful argument and close work with professors. My interests are philosophy of mind, philosophy of AI and technology, the ethics of artificial agents, and epistemology.

Why KU Leuven

To me, KU Leuven is Belgium’s most prestigious university. For philosophy, I am also drawn to the international reputation of the Institute of Philosophy. I enjoy being in Leuven and have friends there. I can see myself studying there one or two days a week, even now that I am part of Wintercircus in Ghent.
