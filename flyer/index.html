The community

Many minds.
One noisy notebook.

Corvus calls its people an army. Think builders backing builders: sharing what they know, opening doors and giving useful feedback. Less marching. More making.

Follow a build, try a game, read a rabbit hole. You can look around before putting your name on anything.

Why bother?

Good friction.
Better questions.

Because a half-built idea benefits from another pair of eyes. Because curiosity crosses disciplines. Because sometimes the person who spots the problem doesn’t write the code.

Gabe builds things and trains BJJ. Both are excellent ways to discover that your first plan needs work.

No guaranteed glory.
Room to roll up your sleeves.
