# Five more episodes — proposals, not booked visits

All episodes start with permission and a specific mutually agreed exchange. Match the language to the owner’s preference and Gabe’s actual ability; don't infer a person's language from appearance.

1. **The börek cross-section.** A bakery with a product that looks better close up than in its current photos. Offer a 15-second dough-to-tray-to-first-bite clip for one agreed portion. Rep: make one texture the whole story. Ask which language they want; Turkish if useful and agreed.
2. **Lahmacun, one continuous take.** Show one real prep-to-plate movement, with the owner's approval. Trade the finished short for one lahmacun. Rep: clear direction and a clean take while staying out of the kitchen's way. Do not claim a speed or cooking time based on an edited sequence.
3. **The quiet-hour coffee counter.** Visit outside the rush and offer a simple “here's how to find us” promo for a drink and agreed snack. Rep: a useful local call to action in the owner's preferred language. No secret filming at the till.
4. **The lunch special nobody knows about.** Ask a small lunch spot to choose the dish and confirm its current price. Offer a menu-specific clip for that meal. Rep: one offer, one price, one clear message. Only include availability the shop confirms.
5. **The second-language redo.** Return to a willing previous participant and compare an original promo with a clearer second version in the language they choose. Agree a new exchange rather than expecting another free meal. Rep: accept feedback and improve the work. Show an honest “no improvement” if that is the outcome.

No fabricated restaurant names, attendance, customer counts, owner reactions or promises of viral reach. A declined trade can become an episode if the person and shop are treated respectfully and any recognizable footage is approved.
