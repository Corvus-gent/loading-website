# I DON'T PAY — Episode 1: earn the first bite

**Length:** 60–90 seconds. **Status:** filming plan, not a filmed episode. **First target:** a Turkish bakery or lahmacun spot in San Francisco; shop not confirmed. **Trade:** one useful 15-second promo in Turkish for a clearly agreed meal. No assumed free food, no guaranteed reach.

**If the answer is no:** smile, put the camera away, thank them. Pay normally if you order, or leave without ordering. Film an honest reflection outside: “First rep: rejected. Fair enough. Next counter.” A failed pitch is a valid episode; never stage an owner's yes or a free meal.

## Shot list

1. Exterior/name, public-facing sign, entrance; avoid visible customers and private addresses unrelated to the shop.
2. Gabe's three-second hook, plus a clean alternate take.
3. Ask the owner off camera first. Only record the conversation after they agree.
4. Owner-approved prep close-up: hands, dough, tray, oven. Stay out of working paths.
5. Börek layers or lahmacun garnish; use the meal that was actually agreed.
6. Plate hero, quick overhead, one movement shot. No stock food masquerading as theirs.
7. In-language promo: record clean voice near the phone, plus 10 seconds of room tone.
8. Show the owner the draft and confirm factual details.
9. Deliver the usable file before calling the exchange complete.
10. Bite, score, actual deal outcome, and next-spot question.

Shoot vertical 9:16. Keep the shop's original promo separate from the Corvus episode so the owner receives a useful standalone video. Use music you have permission to use; clean food/room sound works too.

## Permission and agreement checklist

- [ ] Ask the owner or authorized manager first, before filming inside or ordering on a proposed trade.
- [ ] Agree exactly what you will deliver: a 15-second vertical promo, language, number of revisions and when/how they receive it.
- [ ] Agree the exact meal and whether any part still costs money. A smile is not acceptance of the deal.
- [ ] Ask separately whether the owner is comfortable appearing and speaking in Gabe's public episode.
- [ ] Agree whether/where the shop may post its clip and whether Gabe may post the episode. Don't promise a posting date, reach or results you cannot deliver.
- [ ] Don't film recognizable customers without their consent. Reframe, wait, blur or leave that shot out. Never pressure a customer to participate.
- [ ] Avoid filming minors, payment screens, receipts with personal details, staff schedules or back-of-house material without clear permission.
- [ ] Respect anyone who changes their mind before publication; leave their identifiable footage out.
- [ ] Check the shop name, neighborhood, website, pronunciation and factual claims with the owner.
- [ ] Make the barter explicit in the caption/episode. Do not disguise it as an unrelated review.
- [ ] Owner gets the agreed file; Gabe records the actual outcome and his own honest food score.
- [ ] Publish only after the relevant permissions are clear. These are practical filming steps, not a legal release template.
