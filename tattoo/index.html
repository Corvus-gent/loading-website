The fire in Monferno.

In Sinnoh, Paul gives up on Chimchar, and Ash takes him in. With Ash, Chimchar learns to trust again. It’s a story about what happens to the same fire when someone gives it room to grow.

  1. Chimchar

    The spark. His fire can overwhelm him. Learning to trust Ash is as much a part of his growth as learning to use Blaze.

  2. Monferno

    Still becoming. Chimchar evolves while facing Paul again, and the battle still ends in a loss. I keep coming back to this form. You can have come a long way and still be figuring out what to do with your fire.

  3. Infernape

    Fire with direction. In the Sinnoh League rematch, Infernape channels Blaze and defeats Paul’s Electivire.

For the tattoo, I’d keep Monferno in the foreground. A small ember can carry Chimchar, and an upward flame can hint at Infernape. The curl of Monferno’s tail can tie all three together.

Anakin, beneath the mask.

On Tatooine, a boy dreams of leaving the desert. Years later, that same boy is a Jedi terrified of losing Padmé. Palpatine promises a way to save her. Anakin takes his hand, and the choices that follow turn him into Vader.

Years later, Luke refuses to give up on his father. When the Emperor is about to kill Luke, Vader saves him at the cost of his own life. The last choice he makes is Anakin’s.

For the tattoo, I’d carry one eye through the entire story. The hood holds the fall. The crack in Vader’s mask lets the person underneath come back into view.

The phoenix.

Fall down, get back up.

The phoenix rises from its own ashes. I keep thinking about the moment just before it rises, when there’s ash on the ground and only enough heat left for one ember. Getting back up can start that small.

Monferno is still learning to live with his fire, even after a loss. Anakin has spent years inside the armor when he finally makes a different choice. I see the phoenix in that moment when there’s still something left to act on.

From February to May,
I died.
And came back.

That’s why I’m so hungry right now.

I’ve felt misunderstood my whole life. My solo trip through China and my solo trip to San Francisco and New York changed something for me. Since those trips, I don’t give a fuck what people think of me.

That’s what the phoenix means to me. I can fall and get back up at my own pace. I’m going to conquer the world my way. Solo army if needed.

For the tattoo, start low with scattered ash and a single ember. Let one sweep rise into a wing, leaving a strip of bare skin between the ash and the first feather. That moment of lift is the part I want to keep.
