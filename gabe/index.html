    hi. i’m gabe.

    Founder of Corvus. Belgian builder, storyteller and occasional business philosopher. I write the code, put myself on camera, and find people who want to build something worth caring about.

    Ghent roots · SF stories · NYC sidequestscode · content · community7 languages
    Explore my CV →Book a call →

    what i’m building.

    Corvus is a community for driven people. Builders helping builders, sharing the process and helping each other win.

    I’m building the place as I go: daily games, the Legion, public visitor stats, and plenty of small things that probably didn’t need an easter egg. They got one anyway.

    The crow now has a public logo vote and a tiny Golden Snitch-inspired feather. Think you can outdo me? Send your own design.

    code meets camera.

    I ship products and show how they happen. SF hackathon diaries, robotics interviews, livestreams, and short-form stories across LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

    I use AI tools to turn ideas into working software and raw footage into channel-specific edits. The Compound Effect is part of that experiment.

    The technical side →The content side →

    before this.

    BSc in Computer Science at Ghent University. I left the MSc in Computer Science Engineering in 2026 to build full-time.

    Previously: a GTM internship at Pidgy / Alkmist in October 2026, running a tutoring business, and building websites with fellow students. Pidgy is previous experience; Corvus is what I’m building now.

    beyond work.

    Chasing an IBJJF world title in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. Training most days, learning Chinese, plotting the next surf or hiking trip, and occasionally falling in love with a corgi at a café.

    Still down for a good conversation. Especially if there’s coffee.
