FOUND IN THE SHIP’S LOG · PIER 9

The Historic Klamath.

A Bay Area ferry survived a wartime collision, became a floating design studio, and made it back to San Francisco for another kind of build.

  1. 1924 · Ferry days

    The Klamath carried cars and passengers across the Bay before bridges and freeways took over. The Richmond–San Rafael Bridge ended her ferry career in the 1950s.

  2. World War II · Collision

    The ferry rammed into a submarine during the war. The Klamath survived and went on to outlast the Bay’s original commuter ferry network.

  3. 1964–1987 · A studio afloat

    Designer Walter Landor bought the retired ferry. It became the headquarters of Landor Associates, with visitors including Andy Warhol.

  4. Pier 9 · Back in the Bay

    After three decades in Stockton, the ferry returned to the Bay in 2021 for a refit. It now sits at Pier 9 and hosted Boat Hackathon 2026.
