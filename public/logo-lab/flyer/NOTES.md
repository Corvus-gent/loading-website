# FLYER

A crow has taken Diogenes’ lantern off its hook. Three gouges run with the grain of its wing while the bent feet pull against it.

A builder searching for other builders makes a better street-poster story than a corporate crest. The lantern disappears first at distance. Leave it out at favicon size and print the full cut in a single ink on a cream hoodie.

Type pairing: DejaVuSerifCondensed-BoldItalic with DejaVu Sans Mono. The kit includes independent vector lettering. Palette roles are defined in palette.json.
