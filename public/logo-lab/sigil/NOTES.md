# SIGIL

An open C folds into a bill at its upper terminal. A drilled eye fixes the direction of the bird without closing the counter.

The quiet homepage gains a small, confident signature. This is the least specific to the Legion and can read as a duck. Use the plain symbol at 16px and the construction proof for explaining it on a banner.

Type pairing: DejaVuSans-Bold with DejaVu Sans Mono. The kit includes independent vector lettering. Palette roles are defined in palette.json.
