# LEGIO

A perched crow has become the finial on a Roman standard. A hooked U cut from the hanging cloth doubles as a boarding bridge.

This belongs to earned Legion ranks and a boat hackathon. The leaves and pointed primaries compete in a small avatar. Drop the leaves for favicons and use the full pattern on a woven patch.

Type pairing: DejaVuSerif-Bold with DejaVu Sans Mono. The kit includes independent vector lettering. Palette roles are defined in palette.json.
