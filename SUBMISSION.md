# The Hackers Bay

Luma is sinking. The Hackers Bay is coming.

## Description

The Hackers Bay helps you decide whether an event is worth the trip and gives you three people to meet once you arrive. Choose a persona or paste a public profile to draft an editable crew card. **Broke hacker** checks food, sleep, travel, and prize payouts. **Busy builder** weighs the time against potential collaborators. **Power connector** looks for a reason to make each introduction. **Investor / brand** looks for projects, traction, and sponsor fit.

**Sink a Luma** reads a public listing through any of those lenses, using its published title, date, place, host, and Going count. In the Show Boat Demo Day walkthrough, the sonar locks onto three fictional people and explains why each side might want to meet. Both sides opt in before **IT'S MUTUAL ⚓** reveals a suggested time and place.

With credentials, **Context.dev** reads public profiles, **NYTEX TrustedRouter** routes confidential persona inference, and **Boat.dev** runs approved repos in temporary VMs for Proof of Build. Public event imports and shared intro links work on the live site. The walkthrough uses a fictional roster and sample traction. Profile drafting falls back to heuristics, private mode shows a demo label, and Proof of Build opens a labeled sample when live services are unavailable.

## Straight talk

- Public Luma data is live. Hidden details stay hidden, and an unavailable listing opens a fictional sample.
- The acceptance flow works across two browsers. The Demo Day roster, match reasons, sample traction, and suggested meetups are staged. The event organizers handle official RSVPs.
- Live sponsor calls require provider credentials. The Demo Day experience can run on the local crew-card draft, labeled private-mode demo, and sample sandbox preview.
