I · QUIS SUM

Who stands before the Senate

My name is Gabriel. Belgian, based in Gent, home among the builders of the Wintercircus Collective. I speak seven languages, I studied computer science at Ghent University, and I did go-to-market at Pidgy before I set out on my own.

Last month I rebuilt corvus.gent alone: 700+ commits, 200k+ lines of code, shipped in public. Then I went to San Francisco and New York for ten days on a shoestring, just to meet the people who build. I came back with one conviction: Europe needs that energy, and Corgi can carry it.

II · DIOGENES

The man with the lantern

Diogenes of Sinope lived in a barrel and owned almost nothing. When Alexander the Great, the most powerful man alive, stood before him and offered him anything he wished, Diogenes asked only one thing: that the king step aside, out of his sun.

I am built the same way. I do not need your treasury. I need your name behind me, and room to work.
— Gabriel, Gent

Give me approval and backing, and I'll figure the rest out. That is not a slogan; it is how I've worked my whole life: a lantern, a city and the stubbornness to knock on every door.

III · CUR EUROPA

Why Europe, why now

Rome did not win by sending gold to its borders. It won by sending a trusted legate who already spoke the local tongue, built roads and made allies of strangers.

Europe is twenty-seven provinces, two dozen languages and a thousand small communities who have never heard of Corgi. They need someone on the ground who knows the roads.

Veni, vidi, vici was written in three words. The campaign behind it took years of presence. I'm offering the presence.

IV · QUID DABO

What I bring to the campaign

  • ISeven tongues. Content, calls and communities in the language of each province, not translated, spoken.
  • IIA forum in Gent. The Wintercircus Collective as a base for meetups, hack nights and watch parties that carry Corgi's banner.
  • IIIGo-to-market from the trenches. Growth, distribution and launches at Pidgy; I know how a product spreads from person to person.
  • IVSpeed. I ship daily and in public. You'll see the campaign move before you read a report about it.
  • VThe army. Corvus: builders, makers and quality seekers who help each other first. A legion ready to march.
V · QUID PETO

What I ask

Not a salary. Not a budget. Two things:

  • IApproval: your word that I march for Corgi in Europe.
  • IIBacking: your banner, your name and a direct line when I need it.

The roads, the allies and the victories: I'll figure the rest out.
