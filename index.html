Episode I

The Realm

It is a time of noise. Feeds are full, rooms are empty, and builders everywhere ship alone in the dark.

From an old circus in Ghent, a lone builder named Gabe has raised a banner. Its name is CORVUS.

Its laws are few. Build in public. Help each other win. Have no enemies, only love.

Anyone may enter the Legion. Every rank above it must be earned, never bought.

The army is small. The standard is high. The doors are open, for now.
