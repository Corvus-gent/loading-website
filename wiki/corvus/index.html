A Corvus (Latin: crow) is a black, clever and slightly mysterious bird. In the wild, crows are known for solving puzzles and remembering faces. What the Corvus of corvus.gent does exactly is still unknown. Researchers are watching closely.

History

It started as a small web studio from Ghent, founded by a team of fellow students. The studio built websites with design thinking as priority no. 1. In 2025 the Corvus died. The domain did not. In 2026 it was revived as the portfolio of Gabe, where it still flies around looking for an honest man.

Family

Scientists place crows in the family Corvidae and pigeons in Columbidae. Corvuspedia rejects this. The Corvus and the Pidgy are closely related and belong to the same family: one big fam. Both are birds, both live in Ghent, and both work for the same human.

Behaviour

The Corvus is often seen near barrels and lanterns, see Diogenes. It reacts to keyboards: press any key on the homepage.
