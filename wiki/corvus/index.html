From Corvuspedia, the encyclopedia of corvus.gent

Corvus is the crow of corvus.gent. It first gave its name to a web studio founded by students in Ghent. Today it keeps watch over the realm, a collection of games, music and little rooms behind the portal.

    Death and return

    The studio fell silent in February 2026. The domain stayed up. In November the crow returned to the same address, where visitors can enter the realm and choose their own route.

    Diogenes’ lantern

    The amber light belongs to Diogenes. In the old story he carried a lit lantern in broad daylight, looking for an honest person. That search continues here. On the portal, a tap on the crow reveals a line about its whereabouts.
