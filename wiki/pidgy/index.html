Pidgy is a pigeon that flies into your inbox: pidgy-ai.com. More precisely, it is an AI inbox agent for Outlook and Gmail, built by Alkmist in Ghent. Like real carrier pigeons, it makes sure messages get where they need to go, and that none of them get dropped.

What it does

Pidgy turns email chaos into a short list of what actually needs you. Its natural habitat is pidgy-ai.com. A free trial week can be obtained here.

Family

Although pigeons are officially Columbidae, the Pidgy belongs to the same family as the Corvus: one big fam. The Corvus provides the mystery, the Pidgy provides the inbox relief.

Sightings

Pidgies have been spotted all over corvus.gent: on fire on the army page, covered in sticky notes on /now, and building pages that don't exist yet. Seven of its feathers are hidden on this website.
