Pidgy

From Corvuspedia, the encyclopedia of corvus.gent

Pidgy is the pigeon behind the AI inbox agent built by Alkmist in Ghent. It works in Outlook and Gmail, reading incoming mail and bringing the messages that need a human decision to the top.

    A working day in the inbox

    When a question arrives, Pidgy can draft a reply in the reader’s voice. It also flags conversations that need a follow-up. The reader gets a shorter list of things to act on.

    The pigeon

    Carrier pigeons once delivered letters. On this site Pidgy plays their digital descendant. Look for the bird under sticky notes, on fire, or helping build an unfinished page. In Corvuspedia, Pidgy and the crow belong to the same flock.
