# The Corvus message (use everywhere: site, posts, bios, DMs, Grok)

## The problem
Driven people are often the only one around them who goes this hard. Existing communities are split by sector, performative, or pay-to-play.

## What Corvus is (one line)
**for people who build, in any field.**

## Longer (two lines)
corvus is a hand-picked army of driven people (founders, builders, artists, rappers, skaters, surfers, fighters, climbers) who share what they know, open doors for each other and ship.
no flexing. no pay-to-play. gabe picks every soldier.

## What you get
- people who go as hard as you, across every field
- advice, knowledge and intros from people who've done it
- chances: collabs, gigs, co-founders
- a reason to ship every week (missions, the daily puzzle)

## Ranks vs paying
- ranks are **earned**: helping people, shipping, bringing good people in. never bought.
- paying (tiro → senator on Buy Me a Coffee) **supports corvus** and gives perks (letters, calls, a statue in the realm). it never buys a rank or a seat.
- gabe decides who gets in.

## How to say it in posts
- lead with the person, not the lore: "for people who build, in any field." first, the crow/realm second.
- one link: `corvus.gent/join-the-army-now` (add `/<platform>/<campaign>` for tracking).
- avoid: "pay to rank up", "exclusive club", hype without saying what you get.
- bios: `ceo & general of corvus · for people who build, in any field · corvus.gent/join-the-army-now`
