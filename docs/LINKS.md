## 2. Link-in-bio pages
Pattern: `corvus.gent/links/<persona>/<platform>/<campaign>`. Every click goes out with `utm_source=<platform>`, `utm_medium=links`, `utm_campaign=<persona>-<campaign>`, `utm_content=<button id>`.

| Persona | Buttons | Socials | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gabe` | call, portfolio, now, sidequests | linkedin, ig-startup, ig-bjj, tiktok, x, luma | live |
| `corvus` | games, music, stats, wiki | linkedin (others empty until the accounts exist) | live |
| `pidgy` | alkmist, wiki | linkedin, instagram, tiktok, youtube, facebook | live |

Short form for gabe: `/links/<platform>/<campaign>` (e.g. `/links/ig/bjj`).

## 3. `/go` links (straight to a destination, counted first)
Pattern: `corvus.gent/go/<platform>/<target>/<campaign>`
- Targets: `me` (profile), `home`, `now`, `sidequests`, `support`, `402`, `cv`, `pidgy` (→ pidgy-ai.com with UTMs), `book` (15-min booking), `coffee` (Buy Me a Coffee). Unknown target → `me`.
- Examples: `/go/x/pidgy/shill-inbox`, `/go/li/book/sf-post`, `/go/qr/pidgy/flyer`.

## 7. UTM rules
- `utm_source`: the platform (linkedin, instagram, tiktok, x, discord, luma, buymeacoffee, whatsapp, email, qr, share, self)
- `utm_medium`: post · reply · story · bio · company-page · company-post · links · share · reminder
- `utm_campaign`: always `gabe-<name>`, lowercase with dashes. Only campaigns on the approved list show by name in public stats; the rest count as `other` (raw value on `/private`).
- `utm_content`: the spot; on X `<eu|sf>-<hour>` (e.g. `sf-19`).
